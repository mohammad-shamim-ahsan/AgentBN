---------------------------------------------------
SUCCESS AND FAILURE DEFINITION
---------------------------------------------------

A scenario is successful if:
1. the argmax posterior of "{target_node}" matches the "Ground Truth";
2. its posterior probability is at least {min_confidence}; and
3. its margin over the second-highest posterior is at least {min_margin}.

Otherwise, the scenario is a failure.

## REFINEMENT TASK

The selected baseline BN has already been determined by the workflow.

The refinement recommendation defines the targeted refinement search space.

If "cpt_recommendations" is non-empty:
- Only the recommended CPTs may be refined.
- Within each recommended CPT, only the reported candidate parameters may be intentionally modified.
- Follow each reported adjustment direction ("increase" or "decrease"), while determining the magnitude of change.
- Other states in the same CPT column may be changed only as necessary to preserve normalization.

If "cpt_recommendations" is empty and the baseline BN still contains failures, use the oracle-identified nodes ({oracle}) as the fallback CPT search space and determine candidate parameters from the available evidence.

The graph structure, nodes, edges, states, parent lists, and `parent_state_order` must remain unchanged.

Generate 3 independent refinement candidates representing distinct refinement strategies.

A candidate may refine any subset of the allowable CPTs and parameters. Prefer minimal modifications that correct failures while preserving successes.

Determine the probability modifications using the refinement recommendation, domain context, BN semantics, and recorded failure and success scenarios.

Each candidate will be evaluated independently by the deterministic evaluation pipeline, and the best-performing candidate will be selected for the next iteration.

---
## CPT VALIDITY REQUIREMENTS

Any modified CPT must remain a valid conditional probability table.

Specifically:

- Every column represents a probability distribution over the child states.
- Every probability must lie within [0, 1].
- Every column must sum to exactly 1.
