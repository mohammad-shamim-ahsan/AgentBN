---------------------------------------------------
SUCCESS AND FAILURE DEFINITION
---------------------------------------------------

A scenario is considered successful only if all of the following conditions are satisfied:

1. The predicted root cause, defined as the argmax posterior of "Root_Causes", matches the "Ground Truth";
2. The posterior probability of the predicted root cause is at least 0.50; and
3. The difference between the posterior probability of the predicted root cause and that of the second most probable root cause is at least 0.20.

Therefore, a scenario is considered a failure if any one of the following conditions holds:

1. The predicted root cause does not match the "Ground Truth";
2. The posterior probability of the predicted root cause is less than 0.50; or
3. The difference between the posterior probabilities of the most probable and the second most probable root causes is less than 0.20.

## REFINEMENT TASK

The selected baseline BN has already been determined by the workflow.

The "cpt_danger_report" field is the fault-localization result and defines the allowable refinement search space.

The reported CPTs are plausible refinement candidates identified during fault localization. Only these CPTs may be modified. Every other CPT must remain identical to the selected baseline BN.

Determine whether all or a subset of the reported CPTs requires modification to correct the observed failures. Do not assume that every reported CPT must be modified.

The graph structure, nodes, edges, states, parent lists, and parent_state_order are FIXED and must remain unchanged.

Generate 3 independent refinement candidates.

Each candidate represents one complete Bayesian Network refinement hypothesis. A candidate may modify one or more reported CPTs, depending on the proposed refinement strategy.

Different candidates should represent distinct refinement strategies rather than merely trivial numerical variations of the same parameter modifications.

For each candidate, determine the corresponding parameter modifications for the selected reported CPTs based on a distinct causal explanation of the observed failures, using the domain context, the Bayesian Network semantics, and the recorded failure and success scenarios.

Prefer the smallest sufficient set of parameter modifications that corrects the recorded failure scenarios while preserving the recorded success scenarios.

The recorded failure scenarios should be corrected under the evaluation definition.

The recorded success scenarios should be preserved under the same evaluation definition.

Each candidate will be evaluated independently by a deterministic evaluation pipeline, and only the best-performing candidate will be selected for the next iteration.

## CPT VALIDITY REQUIREMENTS

Any modified CPT must remain a valid conditional probability table.

Specifically:

- Every column represents a probability distribution over the child states.
- Every probability must lie within [0, 1].
- Every column must sum to exactly 1.
