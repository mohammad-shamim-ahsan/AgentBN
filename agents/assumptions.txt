# Assumptions
#
# 1. Every root node is observed in every scenario.
# 2. Every non-root node therefore has a unique active
#    parent-state configuration.
# 3. Observed non-root nodes retain their observed state,
#    but their active CPT column is still recorded because
#    the CPT contributes to inference through the likelihood
#    P(evidence | parents).
# ==========================================================

The assumptions could read:
Every root node is observed in every scenario.
Root evidence nodes are excluded from deterministic activation tracing because they have no parent-state configuration and their prior CPTs do not contribute to CPT localization under hard evidence.
Evidence child nodes retain their observed state, but their active CPT column is recorded because their CPT contributes to inference through the likelihood P(evidence∣parents).
