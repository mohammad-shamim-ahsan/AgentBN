# === Domain Overview ===

## Introduce the Domain

The Bayesian Network models the diagnosis of pulmonary diseases based on patient risk factors, diseases, clinical findings, and symptoms.

The network represents causal relationships among:

1. Patient risk factors
2. Pulmonary diseases
3. Diagnostic findings
4. Clinical symptoms

The network captures probabilistic relationships among patient risk factors, pulmonary diseases, clinical findings, and symptoms.

The network is a benchmark Bayesian Network commonly known as the Asia (Lung Cancer) network.

------------------------------------------------------------

# === Medical Variables ===

The Bayesian Network contains variables representing patient history, diseases, diagnostic findings, and clinical symptoms.

## asia

Indicates whether the patient has recently visited Asia.

Travel history is considered a risk factor for tuberculosis because certain regions have a higher prevalence of the disease.

States:
- yes
- no

## smoke

Indicates whether the patient is a smoker.

Smoking increases the likelihood of several respiratory diseases, particularly lung cancer and bronchitis.

States:
- yes
- no

## tub

Represents whether the patient has tuberculosis.

Tuberculosis is an infectious disease affecting the lungs.

States:
- yes
- no

## lung

Represents whether the patient has lung cancer.

Lung cancer is strongly associated with smoking.

States:
- yes
- no

## bronc

Represents whether the patient has bronchitis.

Bronchitis is inflammation of the bronchial tubes and is more common among smokers.

States:
- yes
- no

## either

Represents whether the patient has at least one of the following diseases:

- Tuberculosis
OR
- Lung cancer

This variable represents whether the patient has at least one of the two diseases. It is introduced because tuberculosis and lung cancer can produce similar clinical findings.

States:
- yes
- no

## xray

Represents the result of a chest X-ray examination.

An abnormal chest X-ray may indicate the presence of tuberculosis or lung cancer.

States:
- yes
- no

## dysp

Represents whether the patient experiences dyspnea (shortness of breath).

Dyspnea may be caused by bronchitis, tuberculosis, or lung cancer.

States:
- yes
- no

------------------------------------------------------------

# === Medical Relationships ===

General causal flow:

Risk Factors
        ↓
Diseases
        ↓
Clinical Findings
        ↓
Symptoms

Travel history influences the likelihood of tuberculosis.

Smoking influences the likelihood of lung cancer and bronchitis.

Tuberculosis and lung cancer may produce abnormal chest X-rays.

Tuberculosis, lung cancer, and bronchitis may all contribute to shortness of breath.

------------------------------------------------------------

# === Important Characteristics ===

The network models uncertainty using probabilistic relationships rather than deterministic rules.

Observed symptoms may have multiple possible underlying causes.

Not every smoker develops lung cancer.

Not every traveler develops tuberculosis.

An abnormal chest X-ray does not guarantee disease.

Dyspnea has multiple possible causes.

The network combines all available evidence to compute the most probable explanation.
